I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.cc/), supervised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://junxianhe.com/). My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**.

## Research Interests

My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** — synthesizing verifiable reasoning data and learning logical reasoning at scale.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — investigating the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding and hallucination mitigation.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — exploring the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs.
